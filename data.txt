CanCap Group is a privately-owned Canadian financial services company providing lending and servicing capabilities across multiple lending verticals. We specialize in direct to consumer and indirect lending channels for automotive finance, consumer unsecured, and merchant lending. Our extensive experience also includes operations in support of mortgage, credit card and small business lending services.
We manage the entire lifecycle of the finance receivable from credit adjudication through to contract administration, customer service, default management and post charge-off recoveries. CanCap was founded in 2011 by highly successful Canadian entrepreneurs and a strong management team that now boasts over 100 years of collective lending and servicing experience. Together, CanCap and its continually growing team of dedicated and motivated individuals pursue common goals of inclusivity, trust, best-in-class customer service.
